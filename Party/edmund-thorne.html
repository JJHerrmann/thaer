Edmund Thorne

Concept: English Elf Wizard, part of the Aegis of Equilibrium's Junior Warden party alongside Gift I. Fills the party's arcane control/research role — scholarly gentleman-mage rather than battle-caster, fitting given the Aegis's own Arcanist role (artifact research) established in Thaer.

Level 1 Ledger

Common Room Recovery — once per day, during a short rest, he can work back a portion of spent spellcasting reserve through sheer bloody-minded study. Named for the Oxbridge college common room he'd rather be in.

Cantrips known:

1st-level spells prepared:
