The Linienwall — real scale

The Linienwall line above is no longer an approximation of any kind — it's the actual 1706 survey, digitized by researcher Maximilian Kaiser (m-kaiser/Vienna-1700 on GitHub) from the real Marinoniplan, a historical map literally titled "...mit dem Linienwall," held by the Wien Museum. All 22 of its valid digitized segments (two tiny fragments far to the north sit outside the wall's real bounding box and were excluded) chain into one real, continuous, genuinely jagged arc spanning the full historical extent; reprojected here from real lat/long into the same coordinate system as everything else on this page. An earlier version of this panel only used 15 of those 22 segments and looked visibly incomplete as a result — that was an error in the reconstruction, not a gap in the survey; this is the corrected full chain. It's drawn open, not closed, because the real data doesn't close either — the wall's two ends both terminate near the Danube Canal (one close to the old city, the other out near Sankt Marx), which finished the enclosure on that side rather than more rampart. The old city star (still a smoothed approximation through four Ringstrasse points, not this same treatment yet) sits inside it. Built in just four months in 1704, one year before the Yawning, on the order of Emperor Leopold I (Josef's own father). Traffic passed through nine real toll gates (each charging the Liniengeld) — Matzleinsdorfer Linie is the one with the best-documented archival record. This is the real boundary Sühnestadt's exile line is drawn against. Note the dashed circle near the top: that's Leopoldstadt, the island between the Danube Canal and the main Danube. It was administratively part of the same Linien toll system, but no wall geometry for it turned up in this source's fortification layers even though the source map covers that ground — most likely because, as an island, its boundary was the rivers themselves plus toll checkpoints at the bridges rather than a built rampart. Marked here as an honest gap, not drawn as a guess.
