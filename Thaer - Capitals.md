# Capitals of the Post-Yawning Empires

## Catholic League — Rome, with a shadow capital at Vienna

The Catholic League's formal seat is **Rome**, as expected. But its political center of gravity in practice sits with the exiled **Archduke Franz Joseph**, styled "the Magnanimous Archduke of Steel," residing in **Stuhnstadt, Vienna**.

*Source: DnD Victorian SCP Campaign, 2023-07-23 (2023-07-31 sub-session)*

Worth flagging: this predates and sits independently of the neutral-Vienna concept already documented on the [[Thaer]] Empire Map and the [Vienna landmark page](Thaer%20-%20Vienna.html) (Vienna as ground claimed by none of the four powers). Whether an exiled Catholic Archduke residing in Vienna is compatible with Vienna's neutrality, or whether one of these ideas supersedes the other, is unresolved — flagged here rather than quietly reconciled.

## Orthodox Empire — Krestgrad, on the Volga

**"Krestgrad"** — a city named for the cross, a potent symbol in Orthodoxy — on the Volga river.

*Source: DnD Victorian SCP Campaign, 2023-07-23 (2023-07-31 sub-session)*

## Protestant Union — Bornholm, in the real Baltic Sea

The council's actual meeting seat is the island of **Bornholm**, a real Danish island in the Baltic — not a fictional or symbolic-only location. The path to that decision is itself worth keeping:

- Initial ideas (2023-07-17): a "floating council" meeting offshore near Berlin/Vilnius/Riga/Tallinn for security from Orthodox attack, or a rotating circuit through Baltic capitals (Copenhagen, Oslo, Stockholm, Helsinki).
- Then narrowed to two real island candidates: the **Åland Islands** (between Sweden and Finland) or **Bornholm**.
- **Bornholm won** specifically for defensibility ("the most defensible when looked at by map") and size (588 km², enough for real land grants to member states) over a smaller initially-considered island, Hesselø.

*Source: Disney-inspired D&D Artifacts, 2023-07-17; Uniting Protestant Powers, 2023-07-20 (2023-07-21 sub-session)*

The island itself is politically structured as **ten radial wedges**, one per member state, radiating from a central meeting house with "spoke" halls representing each capital — with the Huguenots given an enlarged wedge (from Gudhjem to Tjørneby) as a homeland gesture given their real history of persecution and diaspora. The small islands of Christiansø and Frederiksø, a real ferry-linked pair just northeast of Bornholm, tie in as a satellite outpost.

The council's own institutional identity, also from this session:
- **Motto:** "Unity in Faith, Strength in Diversity"
- **Alternative names:** The Protestant Union, The Council of Protestant Nations, The Protestant Alliance
- **Founding date:** 1705 (matching the Yawning itself)
- **Leadership:** rotating among member-state representatives, no single fixed head — consistent with the "no obvious single ruler" structural note already on the [[Protestant Confederacy]] page
- **Named formal meeting place:** "Artzainen Etxea" ("House of the Shepherds," Basque-derived) — a name, not a location; the location is Bornholm above, not the Basque country.

*Source: Uniting Protestant Powers, 2023-07-20 (2023-07-21 sub-session)*

## Islamic Caliphate — Istanbul

**Istanbul.** No further elaboration recovered.

*Source: DnD Victorian SCP Campaign, 2023-07-23 (2023-07-31 sub-session)*

## Co-Prosperity Sphere — Kyōten ("Tenjokyo"), discussed as Lhasa

**"Kyōten"**, also called **"Tenjokyo"** (Celestial Cooperation / Celestial City of Cooperation), was discussed as being **Lhasa**, Tibet's historical capital and traditional seat of the Dalai Lama — chosen for the contrast its high-altitude, remote, spiritually significant character offers against the other four capitals.

*Source: DnD Victorian SCP Campaign, 2023-07-23 (2023-07-31 sub-session)*

## Open

- No capital exists yet for the **Mughal Sultanate**, which is new lore added 2026-08-10 and postdates all of the above sessions by roughly three years.
- No capital exists yet for the **Shia Bloc**, whose very existence as a separate entity from the Sunni Caliphate is itself pinned/unresolved — see [[Sunni Caliphate]].
