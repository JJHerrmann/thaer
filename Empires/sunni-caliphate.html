How this doc was built

Stub page, but the best-sourced of the four theological empires — the DnD Victorian SCP Campaign session develops a specific founding figure for this bloc, not just the structural concept. Core concept, name, and the messianic-Caliph material below are the author's own worldbuilding (not proposed by GPT — see Thaer's "Post-Yawning blocs" section for the full provenance breakdown, including a live naming inconsistency this page inherits — see Open, below). Called "the Grand Caliphate of Islam" in the 2023-07-29 session and "the United Caliphate" in the 2023-07-23 session; "Sunni Caliphate" is the name actually used on the live Empire Map.

Sunni Caliphate

One of the four Post-Yawning theological empires — the Islamic Caliphate uniting the world's various Islamic kingdoms into a single bloc, rather than the many-Sultanates status quo of real history.

Core territory (per the Thaer Empire Map): Ottoman lands (Turkey, Egypt, the Levant, North Africa), Arabia, the Sahel belt, Central Asia, and the Sunni-Islamic regions of Southeast Asia (Indonesia, Malaysia, Brunei).

The messianic Caliph

The richest piece of established lore for this bloc: a Caliph rose to prominence as a messianic figure with real parallels to Jesus — pursuing the same kind of fundamental religious reform, and, tied directly to the Yawning itself, resolving the founding Sunni/Shia succession dispute (the historical argument over the Prophet's rightful successor) rather than inheriting it. The Yawning's occurrence was read as a sign of his divine appointment — comparable to Mahdi (the guided-one) prophecy traditions within real Islamic eschatology, and to miracle-worker/reformer parallels with Christian messianic figures.

This is a genuinely strong, specific hook: a single reformer whose legitimacy is tied to the setting's founding cosmological event, not just political conquest.

Open

  • Naming/scope inconsistency, unresolved as of 2026-08-10. The messianic-Caliph material above implies one unified Caliphate with no live Sunni/Shia distinction — which is what the sourced sessions actually describe. The current Empire Map instead has a separate Shia Bloc (Iran, Azerbaijan, Bahrain) split off from this one, added later without being flagged as new, and it directly contradicts the unification story above. Pinned pending an author decision — see the private note in Thaer's Setting doc. If resolved by merging Shia Bloc back in, this page's name might also need to drop "Sunni" specifically, since a unified Caliphate isn't a Sunni-only institution anymore.
  • No capital, or given name for the Caliph himself, yet.
  • The Mughal Sultanate (India/Pakistan/Bangladesh) is a separate, later, unsourced addition specifically because Mughal emperors wouldn't have deferred to this Caliph's authority — see its own reasoning in Thaer's Setting doc. Worth deciding whether that means the Caliph's unification claim is contested/incomplete rather than universal.
